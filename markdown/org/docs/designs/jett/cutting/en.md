---
title: "Jett Jacket: Cutting Instructions"
---

- **Main fabric**
  - Cut **2 front** mirrored
  - Cut **1 back** on the fold
  - Cut **1 yoke** on the fold (if using)
  - Cut **2 sleeves** mirrored
  - Cut **2 waistband extensions**
- **Lining**
  - Cut **2 front** mirrored
  - Cut **1 back** on the fold
  - Cut **1 yoke** on the fold (if using)
  - Cut **2 sleeves** mirrored
  - Cut **4 pocket bag front**
  - Cut **2 pocket welts**
- **Ribbing**
  - Cut **1 waistband**
  - Cut **2 cuffs**
  - Cut **1 collar**
